Helping the words of Abraham reach more people

Ask Abraham is a small labor of love, made to share the teachings of Abraham Hicks with anyone who could use them.

Why we made this

There are thousands of hours of Abraham’s recordings out there, and when something is weighing on you, the right words can be hard to find.

So we built a simple way to ask in your own words and be taken straight to what Abraham said about it, in his own voice, at the exact moment. When you hit a problem, a worry, a health scare, a money knot, or a tough day with your child, we hope an answer is never more than a question away.

What we hope for

That everyone can find their way back into alignment, and live a happy, fulfilled life.
